This policy covers gig.log, an Android app for independent delivery drivers, as installed from Google Play. Stephen Trotter, an independent developer, publishes it.

gig.log does its work on your phone. It has no account, and it does not sell or share what it reads. The sections below are the same words the app shows under Settings > Data & privacy > Privacy.

{{sections}}

Deleting your data

Everything gig.log keeps is on your phone, and the publisher holds no copy, so there is nothing to ask anyone else to delete. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling, deletes it. Android backup may keep a copy in your Google account until you delete that backup there. Exports and screenshots you saved stay where you saved them.

Children

gig.log is made for delivery drivers, who must be adults to drive for these platforms. It is not directed at children.

Changes

When a statement here changes, the revision number goes up and this page shows the new date. In the app, Settings > Data & privacy > Automation & consent shows the revision in effect when you made each choice.
